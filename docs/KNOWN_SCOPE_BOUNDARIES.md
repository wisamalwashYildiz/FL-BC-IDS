# Known Scope and Provenance Boundaries

1. The exact historical main DP predictive-utility output bundle was not located
   in the current project tree. It is not fabricated or substituted.
2. The 20-round reproduction source/configuration is explicitly reconstructed
   from documented configuration because the dedicated historical snapshot was
   not retained.
3. Mobility, RSU handoff, and packet-level deployment are outside the controlled
   non-IID/participation stress experiment.
4. Numerical DP accounting is learner-stage and preprocessing-conditional.
5. Groth16 is scoped and does not prove the entire XGBoost training computation.
6. Large raw/derived datasets and the 1.1-GB Powers-of-Tau artifact are not
   duplicated in Git; reconstruction information and the exact PTAU fingerprint
   are supplied.
